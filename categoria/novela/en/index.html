Spiritual readingNovelsPoetryEssays and history
  • Quo Vadis: A Narrative of the Time of Nero
    Henryk Sienkiewicz · 1896

    Rome under Nero: the patrician Vinicius falls in love with Lygia, a young Christian, and through her comes to know the persecuted community, Saint Peter and Saint Paul.

  • Emma
    Jane Austen · 1815

    Emma Woodhouse, young, rich and sure of her good eye, spends her time matchmaking in the village of Highbury.

  • Mansfield Park
    Jane Austen · 1814

    Fanny Price, a poor child, grows up in her rich relatives' house at Mansfield Park, shy and nearly invisible.

  • Northanger Abbey
    Jane Austen · 1817

    Catherine Morland, an avid reader of Gothic novels, travels to Bath and then to Northanger Abbey, where her imagination sees mysteries and crimes in every corridor.

  • Persuasion
    Jane Austen · 1817

    As a very young woman Anne Elliot refused Captain Wentworth's proposal, swayed by the advice of a family friend.

  • Sense and Sensibility
    Jane Austen · 1811

    The Dashwood sisters, prudent Elinor and passionate Marianne, are left without fortune after their father's death and move to a modest cottage in Devon.

  • The Ball and the Cross
    G. K. Chesterton · 1909

    Evan MacIan, a Catholic Highlander, and Turnbull, an atheist journalist, agree to fight a duel over a quarrel about God, and the police and public opinion keep stopping them.

  • The Innocence of Father Brown
    G. K. Chesterton · 1911

    Twelve stories in which a short, absent-minded priest, Father Brown, solves crimes that baffle the police of London and Paris.

  • The Man Who Was Thursday: A Nightmare
    G. K. Chesterton · 1908

    Gabriel Syme, poet and policeman, infiltrates the secret council of the anarchists of Europe, whose seven members are named after the days of the week.

  • The Napoleon of Notting Hill
    G. K. Chesterton · 1904

    In a dull future London, a joking civil servant is chosen king by chance and, for fun, gives every borough a medieval banner and halberd.

  • Pride and Prejudice
    Jane Austen · 1813

    Lively, independent Elizabeth Bennet and the reserved Mr.

  • The Wisdom of Father Brown
    G. K. Chesterton · 1914

    A second collection of Father Brown cases: a gang of thieves in the Italian Alps, a duel that never takes place, a murderer who does not exist, an old family curse.

  • Lord of the World
    Robert Hugh Benson · 1907

    Early in the twenty-first century a godless humanitarianism rules the world: euthanasia is routine, the Church is cornered, and Europe, America and the East are heading for war.

  • Ben-Hur: A Tale of the Christ
    Lew Wallace · 1880

    Judah Ben-Hur, a young Jewish prince of Jerusalem, is betrayed by his childhood friend, the Roman Messala: one accident is enough to send him to the galleys and make his mother and sister disappear.

  • Loss and Gain: The Story of a Convert
    Saint John Henry Newman · 1848

    Charles Reding, son of an Anglican clergyman, goes up to Oxford in the midst of the Oxford Movement.

  • Fabiola, or the Church of the Catacombs
    Cardinal Nicholas Wiseman · 1854

    Rome, AD 302, on the eve of Diocletian's great persecution.
