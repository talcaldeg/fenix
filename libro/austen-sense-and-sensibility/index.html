Why read it

It suits anyone wondering how to love deeply without losing judgment, and how to suffer without making others suffer. Elinor is an admirable portrait of self-command and family loyalty. A gentle read that leaves you thinking about the value of temperance.

Summary

The Dashwood sisters, prudent Elinor and passionate Marianne, are left without fortune after their father's death and move to a modest cottage in Devon. There their hearts are tested in opposite ways: one keeps her feelings quiet, the other shows everything. Jane Austen's first published novel.
