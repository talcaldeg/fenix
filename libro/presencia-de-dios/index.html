Why read it

For anyone who works all day and feels there is no time left to pray. Brother Lawrence does not propose more hours of prayer but another way of being in what one already does: washing, cooking, serving. It runs to few pages, and it can be put into practice tomorrow in the ordinary routine.

Summary

Conversations and letters of a Carmelite brother who worked for years in his monastery's kitchen. His teaching is simple: live in God's presence in the midst of daily work, offering little things out of love. Very close to the sanctification of ordinary work.

Quotes

The time of business does not with me differ from the time of prayer.
We ought not weary of doing little things for the love of God, who regards not the greatness of the work, but the love with which it is performed.
