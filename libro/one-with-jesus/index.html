One with Jesus: The Life of Identification with ChristUnder copyright: where to buy it

Paul de Jaegher, S.J. · 1929 · Spiritual reading

Why read it

For the reader who already prays and feels that the bond with Christ has settled into admiration or habit. The book proposes something deeper: letting Him live in us. It is short and can be read in a few sittings, but it rewards a second pass with a pencil in hand, because it asks to be meditated.

Summary

A short, luminous book on the «life of identification» with Christ: letting Jesus, present in the soul by grace, live and love in us. The author insists that this union rests on God's real indwelling in the soul in grace, not on mere friendship or outward imitation, and follows it from its beginnings to its fullness. It closes with the prayer to unite oneself to Jesus found in the prayers section.
