The Wisdom of Father BrownFree EPUB and PDF

G. K. Chesterton · 1914 · Novels

Why read it

For readers who already know Father Brown and want more, and for newcomers who lose nothing by starting here, since every story stands alone. There is humour, irony at the expense of experts without common sense, and a compassion rare in detective fiction. It can be enjoyed at any age.

Summary

A second collection of Father Brown cases: a gang of thieves in the Italian Alps, a duel that never takes place, a murderer who does not exist, an old family curse. With his umbrella and his air of understanding nothing, the little priest takes each riddle apart from the inside, showing that evil is rarely as mysterious as it looks and that sound reason finds what pompous science misses.
